# SPS-WO-11 Literature Scout handoff

**Date:** 2026-08-01

**Search type:** threat-oriented primary-source audit

**Verdict:** conditional pass, confidence `0.65`; not an absence proof.

No reviewed source establishes the exact ordered all-to-all arithmetic-average
versus self-only sign crossover in fixed-horizon distinct captures as declared
environmental correlation length changes relative to collector spacing, with a
matched estimator-risk/action-diversity decomposition.

The nearest mechanism threat is Piro et al. (2025), who already show that
spatially correlated detections can cause clustering and redundancy and that
policy heterogeneity restores movement diversity and coverage. Taylor et al.
(AAMAS 2010) already show that more teamwork can hurt under uncertainty without
communication cost. Aust et al. (2022) show benefits from limited communication
under correlated observations. Zhang, Martinez, and Masson (2015) compare
independent and shared-information multi-robot particle-cue search with an
explicit source correlation length. Sung et al. (2020) optimize a non-additive
unique-target objective under limited communication. Elwin et al. (2020) link
environmental correlation length to distributed estimation and communication
requirements. Mateo et al. (2017) show performance loss under excess
connectivity. Li and Guo (2012), Atanasov et al. (2015), Jiang and Lu (2018),
and Du et al. (2021) further establish source seeking, harmful indiscriminate
sharing, and learned topology.

Therefore the paper may not claim that communication can hurt, teamwork can be
worse without communication cost, correlation causes redundant sensing,
diversity helps search, correlation-aware fusion is new, all-to-all comparison
is new, unique-target accounting is new, or that the result is a universal
phase transition.

The conditional opening is only a prospectively tested benchmark-conditioned
ordered crossover under the fixed message/controller/yield contract, plus a
quantitative matched decomposition. If results show only correlation-related
clustering or diversity loss without the ordered sign law and estimator-side
prediction, kill SPS-C04 as too close to prior work.

The audit used exact-phrase and conceptual searches, official proceedings,
publisher/author primary papers, and citation chains. It was not a systematic
database export and cannot guarantee coverage of paywalled, non-English,
forthcoming, or poorly indexed work.
