# Future Industry Ideas

All entries are hypotheses, not validated markets, customer demand, forecasts, or engineering-readiness claims. No financial estimate is asserted in this cycle.

## SPS-FI-001 — Distributed sensing stress-test suite

- **Market hypothesis:** Teams building local-information robotics or sensor coordination systems may need a controlled way to test when weak environmental structure becomes actionable.
- **Gap hypothesis:** Target users may lack an evaluation that separates weak-signal sensing, passive flux, communication value, and geometry under matched counterfactuals.
- **Possible gateway:** Open benchmark plus reproducible baseline leaderboard; later offer private scenario adapters and evaluation support.
- **Demo:** A browser replay showing the same noise and initial state under null and signal conditions, with first-contact timing and information-budget toggles.
- **Realistic scenario:** Research/engineering evaluation tool first; not a safety-certified deployment product.
- **Required validation:** Ten customer interviews, current procurement alternatives, willingness-to-pay evidence, integration requirements, and data-governance review.
- **Financial model:** Deferred until a buyer, budget owner, pricing unit, and service cost are verified.
- **Icing on the cake:** A “coordination microscope” that identifies whether failure is caused by weak sensing, insufficient communication, policy error, or geometry.

## SPS-FI-002 — Environmental collection digital-twin prototype

- **Market hypothesis:** Environmental monitoring teams may value a simulator for comparing localized collection or sampling strategies under uncertain transport.
- **Gap hypothesis:** It may be difficult to distinguish genuine flow-guided improvement from passive flux or increased swept area.
- **Possible gateway:** One narrowly calibrated case study with a domain partner using their field and vehicle constraints.
- **Demo workflow:** import a flow map; define sensing/action limits; run paired null/signal strategies; inspect interception and coverage diagnostics.
- **Risks:** The current physics is abstract and cannot support microplastics, biological sampling, or field-deployment claims without domain calibration and safety review.
- **Financial model:** Deferred; no TAM, price, adoption, or deployment-volume estimate is supportable yet.

## SPS-FI-003 — Simulation contact-semantics audit

- **Provenance:** P01 logged 28,929 reflection-guarded checks. P02 replaced the guard with exact piecewise specular contact, reduced guarded checks to zero, and changed 0 of 144 first-interception outcomes on the same calibration seeds. This is one correctness replication, not evidence of a widespread product problem or customer demand.
- **Market hypothesis:** Teams using particle, swarm, or interception simulators may need automated checks that event outcomes are invariant to timestep and wall-contact semantics.
- **Gap hypothesis:** Endpoint-only event detection can silently change first-contact metrics even when ordinary unit tests pass.
- **Demo workflow:** upload a simulator trace; replay crossings with endpoint, straight-chord, and exact reflected-path rules; surface changed ownership and event-time diagnostics.
- **Gateway:** open-source reproducibility checker before any commercial claim.
- **Realistic limitation:** one benchmark diagnostic is not evidence of a paying market or general simulator failure.
- **Financial model:** deferred until buyer interviews, integration scope, and willingness-to-pay evidence exist.
- **Icing on the cake:** a provenance certificate that ties every reported event to a contact model, timestep, and deterministic tie key.
