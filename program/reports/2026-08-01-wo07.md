## Explain it simply

We tested whether four collectors perform better when they receive a small
shared team summary instead of equally sized independent information. The
shared version was slightly better on average, but not reliably enough. It won
on four seeds, lost on three, and tied on one. Its average improvement missed
the minimum declared meaningful before opening the results.

Some secondary comparisons looked favorable, but the experiment was designed
as an all-or-nothing joint gate. Accepting only favorable pieces afterward would
move the goalposts. The correct conclusion is that this particular three-number
shared statistic did not earn progression. That is a trustworthy negative
result, not evidence that every possible communication method fails.
