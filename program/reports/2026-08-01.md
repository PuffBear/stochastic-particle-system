## Explain it simply

The previous run showed that a controller with privileged full-state information
could capture about nine more particles than standing still. Today we tested
whether that apparent advantage disappeared when the simulator used smaller
timesteps. It did not: the mean advantage was identical at the original and
half-sized timestep, and close at the finest timestep. No seed reversed the
direction. This makes the endpoint credible enough to test the paper's actual
question next.

That actual question remains unanswered: does a bounded three-number team
summary outperform the same-capacity independent controller? Passing this gate
is analogous to checking that a scale is calibrated before weighing two
treatments; it does not tell us which treatment will win.
