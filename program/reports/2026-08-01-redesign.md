## Simple explanation

The previous policy gave every collector the same average direction. That can
help when all collectors are seeing different noisy versions of essentially the
same flow. It can hurt when collectors occupy regions where the true flow is
different, because averaging mixes incompatible evidence and can make every
collector move similarly.

The redesigned paper asks for the exact transition between those regimes. It
does not ask whether a collection of several new methods works. Its one outcome
is unique team captures, and its one target is a sign-change boundary.

## Exact selected question

For four decentralized collectors exchanging one three-scalar local-velocity
summary per step, at what ratio of field correlation length to nominal
collector spacing does all-to-all averaging change the sign of its effect on
unique team captures by `T=1.34`, relative to capacity-matched independent
estimation?
